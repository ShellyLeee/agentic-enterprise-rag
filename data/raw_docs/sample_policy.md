# Enterprise Employee Policy Handbook

## Vacation Policy

Full-time employees accrue paid vacation time based on tenure and employment status. New
employees accrue 15 vacation days per calendar year, prorated from their start date. Employees
with more than five years of service accrue 20 vacation days per calendar year.

Vacation requests should be submitted to the employee's manager at least two weeks before the
planned absence. Managers approve requests based on business coverage, project deadlines, and
team availability. Unused vacation may carry over for one calendar year, up to a maximum of
five days.

## Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week when their role, team commitments, and
manager approval allow it. Remote employees must remain reachable during core collaboration
hours from 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. in their assigned office time zone.

Teams handling regulated customer data may require additional controls, including approved
devices, private workspaces, and mandatory VPN connectivity. Managers may adjust remote work
arrangements during audits, incident response, or critical launch periods.

## Expense Reimbursement

Business expenses must be reasonable, documented, and submitted within 30 days of purchase.
Employees should attach itemized receipts for travel, meals, software subscriptions, and client
meeting costs. Expenses submitted after 30 days require finance approval.

Air travel should be booked in economy class unless a written exception is approved by an
executive. Meals during business travel are reimbursable up to the daily limit published by the
finance team. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless explicitly approved for a client event.

## Security and Data Handling

Employees must protect company, customer, and employee data according to its classification.
Confidential data may be stored only in approved systems and must not be copied to personal
devices, personal email accounts, or unapproved cloud services.

Suspected security incidents, lost devices, accidental data sharing, or unauthorized access must
be reported to the security team immediately. Employees must complete annual security training
and follow additional handling requirements for regulated, financial, or personally identifiable
information.
